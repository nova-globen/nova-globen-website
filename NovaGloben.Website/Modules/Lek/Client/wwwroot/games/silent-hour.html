How a raid works
  1. Each turn, move one operative one square (tap a neighbouring square, or use the arrow keys), or wait. Then every guard takes one step along his dotted route.
  2. Never step into a tinted square. Never end a turn in a dashed square: it becomes tinted when the guards step.
  3. Walk the Spy onto the intel, then stand both operatives on the two OUT squares.
  4. The Beret can remove one guard: stand next to him, outside his view, and tap him.
  5. Every map is proven solvable. "Best possible" is the shortest route the solver found.
