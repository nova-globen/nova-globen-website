    How it works

    • WASD move, the mouse aims, left click shoots, the arrow keys turn and look. Over Remote Desktop or on a touchpad pick Cursor aim: shoot where the cursor points, push it to the screen edge to turn.
    • Shootdodge: Space or Shift dives the way you move. You're harder to hit in the air and on the floor, and time slows around you while you fly.
    • Bullet time: right click or Q. A bubble opens around you: bullets inside crawl, and everyone else in it moves slower. Kills refill it.
    • R reloads, 1 2 or the wheel swap guns, [ ] change mouse sensitivity. Pick up a shotgun, SMG or rifle; painkillers heal.
    • Shoot the red barrels. Headshots do double. First to 15 kills, or the most after 5 minutes.
