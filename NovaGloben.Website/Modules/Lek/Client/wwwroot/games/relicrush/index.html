      How to play

      • One player starts with the relic. Carry it to your team's shrine to score.
      • You only see your own shrine. The other team's lights up when their carrier gets close.
      • Steal: get next to the carrier and press Space (or right click).
      • Pass: E or left click throws it straight to a teammate in sight, aimed with the mouse or the way you face. Passes can't be intercepted.
      • Dash: Shift, every 3 seconds. Move with WASD or the arrows.
      • First to 3, or the best score after 4 minutes. A tie is a draw. The maze is new every match.
