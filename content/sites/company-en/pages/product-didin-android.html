Installing it

  1. Open this page on the phone or TV and download the file.
  2. When Android asks, allow the browser or file manager that opens it to install unknown apps.
  3. Open the downloaded file and choose Install.

On an Android TV without a browser, install it with adb install from a computer.

What it does

Without signing in, the app opens the public sample: five short films by the Blender Foundation, to browse and watch. Signing in, in the phone's browser, gives your own lists, progress, ratings, the assistant and casting to a TV; it needs an account on the Didin site, and accounts are not open for registration. Signing in on an Android TV that has no browser is not possible yet.

Privacy

The app connects to nova-globen.se and plays films from media.nova-globen.se. Nova Globen AB collects nothing through it: no analytics, no advertising ID. When you sign in, it keeps a sign-in token in Android's encrypted storage on the device; signing out removes it and cancels it at the site. For casting to a TV the app includes Google's Cast library, part of Google Play services, which can send Google data about its use under Google's terms. Android and Google Play services on the device connect to Google on their own, as for any app. How Nova Globen AB handles personal data is in the Privacy Policy.
