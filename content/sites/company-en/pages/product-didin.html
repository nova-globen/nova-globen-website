Didin

A streaming app for a catalogue of films and series: browse and search it, read about each title, and watch in the browser.

What it is

Didin turns a collection of video files into a catalogue you can browse. Each film or series has its own page with its plot, year, genres and people, a series has its seasons in order, and a file plays in the browser from where it is stored. Didin is built on Stomme.

What it does

  • Browse and search

    Search by title, filter by type, genre, year, running time, quality and language, and open a name to see that person's other titles.

  • A page for every title

    Plot, people, seasons and episodes, and the titles that belong with it.

  • Watch in the browser

    With subtitles where a file has them, and, signed in, on from where you stopped.

  • Your own lists

    Signed in, you keep a list of what to watch, mark what you have seen and rate it.

  • Ask Didin

    Signed in, you can describe what you are in the mood for, and an AI assistant suggests titles from the catalogue.

  • Cast to a TV

    Signed in, send what you are watching to a Chromecast or Google TV on the same network.

Try it

Didin is in development. A sample is open to everyone, with no sign-in: five short films by the Blender Foundation, shared under Creative Commons licences, each with its credits.
