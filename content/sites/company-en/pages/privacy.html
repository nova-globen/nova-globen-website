1. What personal data we process

1.1 When you visit the website

To deliver and protect the website, technical data about your visit is processed: your IP address, browser and device information, the date and time of the request, and the page requested. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies; the cookies it does set are listed on the Cookies page.

We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

1.2 When you contact us

When you email us or use the contact form, we process what you send: your name, email address, company name if you give it, and your message. A message sent through the contact form is delivered to our mailbox by email; the website does not keep a copy of it.

1.3 When you become a customer

For customers we process contact details, contract and order details, project communication, invoices, payment references and accounting records.

Please do not send sensitive personal data, such as health data, political opinions, religious beliefs, union membership, criminal-offence data or identity documents, unless we specifically ask for it and there is a clear reason.

Payment card details are handled by Revolut on Revolut-hosted payment pages. Nova Globen AB does not collect or store card details on this website. We may receive payment-related information such as payment status, transaction reference, name, email address, amount, currency and invoice information.

Some providers may process personal data outside Sweden or the EU/EEA. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

7. AI-assisted development

Nova Globen AB may use AI-assisted tools when developing software or drafting text. When we do, we aim to minimise personal data: we do not intentionally submit payment card details, identity documents or sensitive personal data to AI tools. If your project needs confidential treatment beyond this, tell us before sending detailed materials.

8. Your rights

Depending on the situation and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, or restriction of processing; to object to processing based on legitimate interest; to receive certain data in a structured, machine-readable format; and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact info@nova-globen.se. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some data cannot be deleted immediately if we must keep it for legal, accounting, tax, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), or with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. When Nova Globen AB acts as a processor

For some business customers, Nova Globen AB may process personal data on the customer's behalf, for example when hosting or supporting software that handles personal data controlled by that customer. In those cases the customer is normally the controller and Nova Globen AB the processor, and a Data Processing Agreement may be required.

10. Visitors in the United Kingdom and outside the EU/EEA

If you are in the United Kingdom, this Privacy Policy applies to you under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and you may also lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

If you are outside the EU/EEA and the United Kingdom, your local data protection laws may apply in addition to this policy. We aim to apply the same standard of care to all personal data we process, wherever you are.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We update this Privacy Policy when our services, providers, legal obligations or processing change. The latest version is published on nova-globen.se with the date shown at the top.
