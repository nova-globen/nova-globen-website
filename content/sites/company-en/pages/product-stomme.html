Stomme

A .NET application platform that gives a business application, or the AI agent building it, everything it needs from the start: sites and tenants, sign-in, content, data storage, automation, AI and integrations. The work goes into the product, not the plumbing.

Why it exists

Stomme is meant to be the foundation a developer, or an AI agent, builds an application on, and to include as much of what an application needs as it can. When applications are built with AI agents, a workflow engine, connectors and an AI assistant are basics rather than separate products. Because people still use what gets built, stability, usability and a good user experience come before the number of features.

It is for teams, and the AI agents working for them, who build business applications on .NET.

What it does

Stomme ships with all of this and needs no outside service or CDN to run. Much of it stays off until you choose it, including what runs code, keeps extra data or costs money; where something needs turning on or setting up, its card says so.

  • Many sites, one installation

    Many sites and tenants run in one installation, each found by its host name or a folder under it. A tenant has its own database or shares one, with its data kept apart.

  • Three ways to run Blazor

    A site runs as static server rendering, Blazor Server or WebAssembly.

  • Your choice of database

    SQL Server or Azure SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite or RavenDB, chosen at installation. A standard module writes no persistence code, and a conformance test suite checks that the relational and document databases behave the same.

  • Sign-in and accounts

    Each tenant has its own sign-in, with sessions a person can see and revoke and an audit of identity events. Each site can set up external OpenID Connect, passkeys, login links and e-mailed two-factor codes. Turn on a tenant's own OpenID Connect provider, so its phone, desktop and TV apps sign people in, and SAML 2.0 and Windows sign-in.

  • Content and pages

    Pages built from modules and themes, rich text, files, search, URL mappings, a recycle bin and sitemaps, all managed from the admin pages. The interface ships in English, and a site can be localised with uploaded translation packages.

  • Automation

    Background jobs and a catalogue of more than 180 connectors. Turn on pipelines for a visual editor that imports n8n workflows and lists anything it cannot carry over, and collaboration so that people editing the same pipeline are told about each other.

  • Data and dashboards

    A catalogue of datasets with their schemas, and dashboards of charts, grids and figures built on them. Turn on data export for CSV, Excel, JSON and PDF.

  • AI assistant and MCP

    Turn on an AI assistant for a site's users. Turn on publishing over MCP, and an AI tool outside the browser reaches a site as the person who made its token, limited to that site, the token's scopes and that person's own permissions.

  • A person's data

    Deleting an account says what it erases, what stays without the name and for how long, and modules declare their part. Retention periods are settings. Turn on portability, and a person can get a copy of their data.

  • Running it

    Health checks, in-app notifications, e-mail through a mail server you set, and upgrades that run at start-up. Set up webhook notifications, and turn on health alerts, usage metering and quotas, and backup and restore of a whole installation.

  • Modules and themes

    An application adds its own modules and themes as separate packages, without changing the framework. The Create Module and Create Theme pages generate one that builds and installs.

  • Ready for AI agents

    A new application starts from a template that every release builds and runs. It brings the guidance an AI agent needs (instructions, documents and skills) on what Stomme already provides, so the agent builds on it instead of beside it.

  • Surveying a repository

    Two optional packages. Conductor runs a playbook with context: units of work, a run that can resume, readers and a judge over an AI model you set up, spending ceilings, questions for a person and a decision log with evidence. Cartographer uses it to survey a software repository and write what a tracker and a wiki can take in: work items, documents, decisions and the links between them.
