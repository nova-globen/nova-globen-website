Stomme

A .NET application platform that gives a business application, or the AI agent building it, everything it needs from the start: sites and tenants, sign-in, content, data storage, automation, AI and integrations. The work goes into the product, not the plumbing.

Why it exists

Stomme is meant to be the foundation a developer, or an AI agent, builds an application on, and to include as much of what an application needs as it can. When applications are built with AI agents, a workflow engine, connectors and an AI assistant are basics rather than separate products. Because people still use what gets built, stability, usability and a good user experience come before the number of features.

It is for teams, and the AI agents working for them, who build business applications on .NET.

What it does

  • Many sites, one installation

    Many sites and tenants run in one installation, each found by its host name, with each tenant's data kept apart.

  • Your choice of database

    SQL Server or Azure SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite or RavenDB, chosen at installation. A conformance test suite checks that the relational and document databases behave the same.

  • Sign-in built in

    Each tenant has its own sign-in, and can act as its own OpenID Connect provider; external sign-in through OpenID Connect, SAML 2.0 and Windows.

  • Automation

    A visual pipeline editor for automations, with a catalogue of more than 180 connectors, that imports n8n workflows and lists anything it cannot carry over.

  • Modules, themes and a template

    Extend it with modules and themes, or start a new application from its template, which every release builds and runs before it ships.
