Security updates

Vacha receives security updates until at least 31 December 2031, free for everyone. Report a vulnerability privately to info@nova-globen.se.

Support agreements

Companies that want priority fixes or help with deployment can buy a support agreement. It buys help and priority, not permission to use Vacha, which the licence already gives. The price is on request, excluding VAT: ask through the contact form or at info@nova-globen.se.
