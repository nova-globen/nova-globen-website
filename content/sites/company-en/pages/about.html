About Nova Globen AB

Nova Globen AB is a software consulting company based in Sweden. We help companies build, modernize and maintain business-critical software with clear communication, pragmatic architecture and long-term maintainability.

What we do

We provide software consulting, cloud solutions and digital product development for businesses: solution architecture and implementation, modernization and maintenance, integrations and automation, and AI enablement. We also build our own products: Stomme, Fravashi and Vacha.
