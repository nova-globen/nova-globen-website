About Nova Globen AB

Nova Globen AB is a Swedish company that builds and sells its own software products.

What we do

We make four products: Stomme, a .NET application platform; Fravashi, a studio where AI coding agents work under a person's review; Vacha, a text and code editor for Windows; and Didin, a streaming app for films and series. We sell licences and support for them, and write technical articles on .NET and Azure.
